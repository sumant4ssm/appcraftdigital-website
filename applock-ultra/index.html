Privacy, ads and permissions

What AppLock Ultra keeps on your phone, what it shares, and why it asks for each permission. The privacy policy has the full detail.

Kept on your phone
Your PIN and pattern (stored only as a one-way hash, and never backed up), intruder photos and hidden notifications. All are encrypted with a key kept in your phone's secure hardware, so if Android backs up the app, those copies can't be read on any other phone.
No account
There's nothing to sign up for. AppLock Ultra works as soon as it's installed.
Ads
The free version shows ads, including on the lock screen, from Google AdMob and its partners Liftoff and InMobi, with a consent form where required. Ad networks may use your approximate location. Premium removes all ads.
Analytics and crash reports
Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics help us fix crashes and see which features are used. Events don't include the names of the apps you lock.
Permissions
Usage access and display over other apps to show the lock, an exemption from battery optimisation so Android doesn't stop it, and notifications for intruder and new-app alerts. Optional: camera for intruder selfies, notification access to hide previews, location for Wi-Fi profiles, device admin for uninstall protection, and accessibility, which setup asks for on tablets.
Purchases
Premium is bought through Google Play and verified with our server. Plans are weekly, monthly, yearly or a one-time lifetime purchase. New subscribers can try Premium free for 15 days.

Questions

How do I lock apps with fingerprint on Android?

Install AppLock Ultra, pick the apps to protect, choose Fingerprint as your unlock method and set a backup PIN or pattern, then allow display over other apps and usage access. Opening a locked app then asks for your fingerprint, with your PIN or pattern as a backup.

Is AppLock Ultra free?

Yes. Locking unlimited apps, every unlock method, hiding notifications, the intruder log and the Quick Settings tile are free, with ads. Premium removes ads and adds lock profiles, intruder selfies, the disguise tools, timed pause, a 30-day notification inbox and extra themes. New subscribers can try Premium free for 15 days.

Which app locker takes an intruder selfie?

AppLock Ultra Premium takes a photo with the front camera after 1, 2, 3, 5 or 10 wrong attempts and adds it to the intruder log. The photo is encrypted and stays on your phone. The intruder log itself is free.

Can I lock WhatsApp, Gallery and banking apps?

Yes. You can lock any app on your phone, with no limit. The same PIN, pattern or fingerprint unlocks every locked app, and notifications from locked apps can be hidden so message previews stay private.

Can I hide AppLock Ultra on my home screen?

With Premium you can replace its icon and name with one of nine everyday apps, such as Calculator, Clock or Weather, and show a fake crash message in front of the lock screen.

Does AppLock Ultra drain my battery?

AppLock Ultra runs a small background service that checks which app is on screen, and checks far less often while the screen is off. It asks to be excluded from battery optimisation so Android doesn't stop it and leave your apps unlocked.
