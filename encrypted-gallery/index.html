Encryption

Encryption

Encrypted, not just hidden

Many "hide photo" apps just move files somewhere less obvious. Encrypted Gallery encrypts every photo, video and file as it enters the vault, so without your unlock it's unreadable data. Large videos are encrypted in small chunks, so they can stream and skip without being decrypted to storage.

Cipher
AES-256-GCM
Large files
Encrypted in 1 MiB chunks
Tampering
A modified file is refused instead of opened

Encryption

Key locked by your PIN

The key that encrypts your files is itself locked with a key made from your PIN, password or pattern, then sealed again with Android Keystore. A second copy is locked with your recovery code, so a forgotten PIN can be reset. Changing your PIN later doesn't need to re-encrypt anything.

Key derivation
PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256, 210,000 rounds
Second layer
Android Keystore
Recovery
120-bit recovery code

Encryption

Encrypted names and thumbnails

File names and details live in an encrypted database, thumbnails are encrypted on disk, and decrypted previews exist only in memory and are cleared the moment the vault locks.

File list
SQLCipher-encrypted database
Previews
In memory only, cleared on lock

Encryption

Screenshot and Recents blocking

Screenshots and screen recordings are blocked everywhere in the app, and it's left out of the Recents screen, so nothing from the vault shows up when you switch apps. This is always on.

Auto-lock

Wrong-attempt lockout

Guessing is slowed down after five wrong attempts. The attempt counter is itself encrypted, and tampering with it locks the screen instead of resetting the count.

02 / Privacy

Ads · Analytics · Permissions

Privacy, ads and permissions

What Encrypted Gallery keeps on your phone, what it connects to, and why it asks for each permission. The privacy policy has the full detail.

Stays on your phone
Your photos, videos, files, notes, PIN and recovery code are never sent anywhere. The vault is excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup.
No account
There's nothing to sign up for, and no server holds a copy of your vault.
Ads
The free version shows Google AdMob ads, with a consent form where required. Pro removes all ads.
Data collected
We run no analytics or crash reporting of our own. In the free version, Google's ads SDK collects device and advertising IDs, app interactions, diagnostics and approximate location, for ads, analytics and fraud prevention. Firebase Remote Config, used only to tell the app when an update is required, uses an installation ID.
Problem reports
Report a problem opens your own email app with a report you can read before sending. It never includes your files, file names, PIN or recovery code, and the app uploads nothing itself.
Permissions
Camera for the private camera and intruder selfies, microphone for the recorder, media or All files access to import items and remove the originals, and notifications to show backup and restore progress. Internet is used only for ads, purchases and the update check. Biometrics are optional.
Purchases
Pro is bought through Google Play and verified with our server. Plans are weekly, monthly, yearly or a one-time lifetime purchase.

03 / Questions

Answers before you install

Questions

How do I encrypt photos on my phone?

Add the photos to Encrypted Gallery. Each one is encrypted on your phone with AES-256-GCM as it enters the vault, and can only be viewed inside the app after you unlock it. The app then offers to delete the unencrypted originals from your gallery.

How do I hide photos on Android?

Import them into Encrypted Gallery and choose to delete the originals when asked. On Android 11 and later you can allow All files access once, so removing originals takes a single tap instead of a confirmation each time.

Is Encrypted Gallery free?

Yes. Encryption, the gallery, players, camera, screenshot blocking, the recovery code and restoring a backup are free, with ads, for up to 20 items, 3 folders per section, 3 notes and 3 voice recordings. Pro removes ads and limits and adds biometric unlock, the decoy vault, intruder selfies, disguise, the photo editor and making encrypted backups.

What if I forget my PIN?

Use your recovery code. You save it when you set up the vault, and with it you can choose a new PIN, password or pattern. Your files stay just as they are. The same code opens your backups on a new phone.

Is my vault uploaded to the cloud?

No. Your photos, files, notes and PIN never leave your phone, and the vault is excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup. If you make a backup yourself (Pro), it is saved still encrypted to a file or folder you choose.

What if someone makes me unlock it?

With Pro you can set up a decoy vault: a second PIN, password or pattern that opens a separate, harmless vault. You can also have the vault wiped automatically after 3 to 20 wrong attempts.

Can I unlock it with my fingerprint?

Yes, with Pro, on phones with strong biometrics. The unlock key is tied to your phone's hardware and stops working if a new fingerprint or face is added, so your PIN is needed again.
