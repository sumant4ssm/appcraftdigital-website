Privacy, ads and permissions

What Internet Velocity keeps on your phone, what it connects to, and why it asks for each permission. The privacy policy has the full detail.

Stays on your phone
Your speed readings, usage history and settings. There's no Internet Velocity server, so nothing is sent to us.
Blocker traffic
The VPN only receives traffic from apps you chose to block, and drops it on the phone. Nothing is forwarded, inspected or logged.
Ads
The app is free and shows Google AdMob ads, with a consent form where required. Google processes device and ad data to serve them.
No analytics
No analytics or crash-reporting tools are built in, and there's no account to create.
Permissions
Notifications for the live meter, usage access for per-app data, display over other apps for the overlay, and VPN for the blocker. Each is asked for only when you use that feature.
Battery
The meter runs as a foreground service. The app may ask to be excluded from battery optimisation so Android doesn't stop it.

Questions

Is Internet Velocity a speed test?

No. It is a live speed meter: it shows how fast data is actually moving through your phone right now, using Android's own traffic counters. It doesn't download test files or contact a test server, so it reads near zero when nothing is using the connection.

How do I see which app uses the most data?

Open the Apps tab and allow usage access when asked. Internet Velocity ranks your apps by data used in the last 24 hours, with download and upload for each, and a chart of the top five.

Can I show internet speed in the status bar?

Yes. Turn on the status bar indicator and the small icon at the top of your screen becomes a live speed readout. A silent notification also shows download and upload speed, and an optional floating overlay can show it on top of other apps.

Can I block internet access for a specific app?

Yes, without root. Choose the apps, switch on blocker mode and allow the VPN request. Their traffic goes to a local VPN on your phone that drops it, while every other app keeps working. Watching two short ads unlocks the blocker for 24 hours.

Does the blocker send my traffic anywhere?

No. The VPN runs entirely on your phone and only receives traffic from the apps you chose to block, which it discards. Nothing is forwarded to a server or logged.

Does it work on Wi-Fi and mobile data?

Yes. The meter works on Wi-Fi, mobile data and Ethernet, the notification splits data used by Wi-Fi and mobile, and the history can be filtered to either one.
