How we build our apps

Four things that hold for every AppCraft Digital app.

Your private data stays on your phone
PINs, photos, files, notes, intruder photos and usage history are stored on the device, and the sensitive ones are encrypted. None of it is uploaded to us.
No accounts
Install and start. There's nothing to sign up for and no profile on a server to leak.
Free, with clear upgrades
Every app is free on Google Play and supported by ads. Paid plans remove the ads and add extra features, and each app page marks exactly what's included.
Open about what's inside
Each app page lists the ads, analytics and permissions that app uses, so you know before you install.
