Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Sheetfox: PDF & Doc Scanner ("Sheetfox") is made by AppCraft Digital. This policy says what Sheetfox does with information. In short: your documents stay on your phone.

Your documents stay on your phone

Everything you scan or import, and its pages, text, names, notes, tags, folders, signatures and the Private folder, is kept only on your phone. Sheetfox's scanning, filters, clean-up and text reading run on the phone. Nothing of it is sent to us. A document leaves your phone only when you share, save, email or print it, to the place you choose. The Private folder is encrypted on your phone with your PIN; we never see the PIN. Backups you make are saved as a file on your phone.

What is sent, and why

  • Finishing setup. The first time Sheetfox is online it finishes setting up with our server. It sends a Google Play Integrity token (Google's check that this is a genuine copy of Sheetfox on a genuine device) and a key made on your phone, so what our server sends back can only be used on your phone. The same check is done again about every three weeks and after each update, only while your phone is online. Being offline never undoes the setup; if a later check does not pass, Sheetfox works as it did before setup until a check passes again.
  • Sheetfox Pro. If you subscribe, Google Play handles the payment; we never see your card. To check the subscription, Sheetfox sends its Google Play purchase token to our server, which asks Google Play whether it is active. This is done when you buy or restore Pro, and when the app starts while Pro is on.
  • Updates. Sheetfox uses Google Firebase Remote Config, which uses a Firebase installation ID, to know whether a version is too old to use; and Google Play to offer updates.
  • Crash reports and app diagnostics. Sheetfox uses Google Firebase Crashlytics. When the app crashes, or runs into an error it recovers from, it sends a report: where in the app's code it happened, the app version, the phone's make and model, its Android version and similar technical details, with a Crashlytics installation ID. Reports never include your documents, their names or their text.
  • Usage statistics. Sheetfox uses Google Analytics for Firebase to count how the app is used: app starts, sessions and the screens opened, purchases of Sheetfox Pro (the plan and its price), and whether enhanced scanning works on your phone and, when it doesn't, why (for example no internet yet, or a phone that doesn't pass Google's security check). Google Analytics uses an app-instance ID and estimates your approximate location (country, region and city) from your IP address. Sheetfox doesn't use your phone's advertising ID, and nothing is used for ads.
  • Text reading. Sheetfox reads text with Google's ML Kit on your phone. ML Kit sends Google information about the device, the app and how the feature performed (diagnostics and usage analytics), with a per-installation identifier; not the text or the pages.
  • Reviews. Google Play may show its rating card; your rating goes to Google Play.
  • Reporting a problem. "Report a problem" opens your own email app with details you can read and change before you send them. We receive them only if you send the email.

Our servers use these only to answer the request, and keep only short-lived technical logs that are not linked to your name or account. Crash reports and usage statistics go to Google Firebase, where we see totals and technical reports. They are not linked to your name, email address or Google account, and are not used to follow you across other apps or websites, or for advertising. We do not sell your information or share it with anyone else, and there are no ads in Sheetfox.

Google's services

Google Play, Play Integrity, Play Billing, Firebase (Remote Config, Crashlytics and Analytics) and ML Kit are Google services, under Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children

Sheetfox is not made for children under 13.

Deleting your information

Uninstalling Sheetfox deletes everything it keeps on your phone (make a backup first if you want to keep your documents). Nothing we receive is linked to you; to ask about it, email us. Crash reports are kept for 90 days. Usage statistics are kept for 2 months, and what is linked to an app-instance ID for up to 14 months after the app was last used; after that only totals that identify no one remain.
