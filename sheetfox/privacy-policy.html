Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Sheetfox: PDF & Doc Scanner ("Sheetfox") is made by AppCraft Digital. This policy says what Sheetfox does with information. In short: your documents stay on your phone.

Your documents stay on your phone

Everything you scan or import, and its pages, text, names, notes, tags, folders, signatures and the Private folder, is kept only on your phone. Sheetfox's scanning, filters, text reading and AI clean-up run on the phone. Nothing of it is sent to us. A document leaves your phone only when you share, save, email or print it, to the place you choose. The Private folder is encrypted on your phone with your PIN; we never see the PIN. Backups you make are saved as a file on your phone.

What is sent, and why

  • Setting up the AI, once. The first time Sheetfox is online it asks our server for the key that opens its AI models. It sends a Google Play Integrity token (Google's check that this is a genuine copy of Sheetfox on a genuine device) and a key made on your phone, so the answer can only be opened on your phone. It is done again only when the models change.
  • Sheetfox Pro. If you subscribe, Google Play handles the payment; we never see your card. To check the subscription, Sheetfox sends its Google Play purchase token to our server, which asks Google Play whether it is active. This is done when you buy or restore Pro, and when the app starts while Pro is on.
  • Updates. Sheetfox uses Google Firebase Remote Config, which uses a Firebase installation ID, to know whether a version is too old to use; and Google Play to offer updates.
  • Text reading. Sheetfox reads text with Google's ML Kit on your phone. ML Kit sends Google information about the device, the app and how the feature performed (diagnostics and usage analytics), with a per-installation identifier; not the text or the pages.
  • Reviews. Google Play may show its rating card; your rating goes to Google Play.
  • Reporting a problem. "Report a problem" opens your own email app with details you can read and change before you send them. We receive them only if you send the email.

Our servers use these only to answer the request, and keep only short-lived technical logs that are not linked to your name or account. We do not sell or share any information, and there are no ads and no tracking in Sheetfox.

Google's services

Google Play, Play Integrity, Play Billing, Firebase and ML Kit are Google services, under Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children

Sheetfox is not made for children under 13.

Deleting your information

Uninstalling Sheetfox deletes everything it keeps on your phone (make a backup first if you want to keep your documents). Nothing we receive is linked to you; to ask about it, email us.
